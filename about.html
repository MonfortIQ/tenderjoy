The Genesis

Reclaiming the Purest Drink on Earth

In modern cities, packaged beverage aisles are stacked high with chemical concentrates, synthetic flavorings, artificial sweeteners, and preservatives masquerading as "health drinks."

TenderJoy was founded in 2024 to re-introduce people to what natural hydration truly tastes like. No pasteurization. No high-fructose corn syrup. No water dilution. Just raw tender coconut water tapped straight from the green shell, and pristine fruits cold-pressed at raw cellular vitality.

Today, TenderJoy operates 5 boutique juice bars in Bengaluru and supplies thousands of fresh coconuts for corporate wellness initiatives, luxury weddings, and daily morning runners.

Our Process

The 5-Step Farm-To-Glass Journey

How we bring raw coconut water from the trees of Pollachi to your hands without losing a single trace mineral.

1

Organic Cultivation

Grown in deep red loam soils using natural composting, drip irrigation, and zero chemical insecticides.

2

7-Month Maturity

Harvested strictly when the water sugar-to-electrolyte curve peaks and tender malai begins to form.

3

Cold Transport

Insulated transit vehicles maintain stable cool temperature to protect heat-sensitive natural enzymes.

4

Diamond Trimming

Outer fibrous husk is cleanly carved into our signature lightweight diamond shell using sanitized blades.

5

Tapped On Order

Punctured right in front of you. Served with a biodegradable straw or poured into reusable glassware.

Food Safety First

Surgical Standards In Every Stall

Traditional street coconut stalls often suffer from dirty knives, dust exposure, and stagnant cutting blocks. We revolutionized the experience with industrial-grade hygiene.

  • Food Grade 304 Stainless Steel: All cutting surfaces and cleavers are autoclaved and sanitized continuously.
  • Zero Contact Opening: Handlers wear food safety nitrile gloves and sanitized aprons at all times.
  • FSSAI & Lab Audited: Regular microbial testing ensures 0% bacterial contamination and 100% pure TDS values.
