Our Story

Born From a Love for Unaltered Botanical Hydration

TenderJoy began with a simple revelation: in a bustling city filled with artificial syrups, packaged concentrates, and plastic-bottled sweetened drinks, the most luxurious beverage in the world has always grown right on our native trees.

We partnered directly with heritage coconut growers in Pollachi and coastal Karnataka to guarantee the sweetest young green coconuts, picked exactly between the 7th and 8th month of maturity when the water is sweetest and the flesh is delicately jelly-soft.
