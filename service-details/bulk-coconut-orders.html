Wholesale & Direct Farmgate

Bulk Coconut Orders

When you need tender coconuts by the hundreds or thousands, quality variance and delivery delays can jeopardize your large-scale event. TenderJoy operates direct grove procurement infrastructure that handles wholesale volumes with precision. Every batch is graded by size, shell density, and sugar content, then dispatched in climate-controlled trucks directly to your venue.

Why Partner With TenderJoy For This Service?

Direct-from-source wholesale pricing without intermediary distributor markups

Stringent quality grading ensuring uniform weight, high water volume, and zero leakers

Fleet of dedicated trucks capable of delivering up to 15,000 coconuts in a single shipment

Flexible packaging: heavy-duty crates, jute bags, or pre-trimmed loose pallet loads

How Our Service Operates

From initial enquiry to post-event clean execution, our operations team ensures a seamless experience.

1

01. Volume & Date Lock

Confirm quantity, preferred harvest grade (sweet dwarf vs coastal green), and delivery date.

2

4

04. Direct Truck Delivery
